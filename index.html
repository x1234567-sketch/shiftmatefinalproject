01 / THE PROBLEM

Busy shifts make little jobs easy to miss.

ShiftMate is designed for owners and shift managers of independent Irish cafés with around 3–10 employees. The café owner would decide whether to pay; shift managers would organise work and staff would check tasks during the day.

“When we're busy serving customers, it's difficult to remember which jobs have been done and which still need attention.” Illustrative invented quote, not a real customer testimonial.

Today, a small café might rely on paper checklists, a whiteboard and verbal reminders. These can make it hard to see who is responsible and what remains unfinished. This is a proposed scenario, not a finding from customer research.

04 / BUSINESS CASE

Why this approach?

Existing alternatives

Trello offers general task boards and cards. SafetyCulture provides digital operational checklists and inspections. These are real services to compare with ShiftMate; verify their relevant features directly on their official sites and record the date you checked them before submitting.

ShiftMate's proposed focus is narrower: a lightweight, phone-friendly checklist for a small café shift. This does not mean other products cannot be used in cafés.

How could it be sustained?

Assumption: a future full product might charge €9.99 per café per month. The prototype is free and takes no payments. The proposed fee is not validated by customer research.

Privacy and limitations

No real employee, customer or employer data should be entered. Data stays in this browser's local storage and can be cleared by browser settings. This demo has no login, cloud backup or multi-device collaboration.

User testing

Testing evidence to complete after a real test: Ask someone who did not build ShiftMate to add a task, mark it complete, refresh, and find an overdue task. Record what they found confusing, what actually broke and what you changed. Do not claim a test happened until it has.
